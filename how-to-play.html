How to play

The aim

  • Build a reef with the level's hexas, one at a time
  • Grow zones of the same colour to score points
  • Meet the goals for ★ stars and reach the points target for the ● pearl
  • Attract fish to fill your collection

Build a reef

  • Pick one of the 3 hexas in hand
  • Its place refills from the hidden pile
  • Place it on any empty spot next to your reef
  • Tap "hexas left" to see every hexa still to come

Rotate & place

  • Tap the hexa on the board to turn it
  • Tap the check mark on the hexa, or Place, to put it down
  • Undo takes it back

Zones score points

  • Touching edges of the same colour form a zone
  • Points = size²: a 4-hexa zone scores 4² = 16
  • Fully enclosed: ×2, so 4 hexas score 32

Bonus points

  • Current: +10 per hexa of your longest current
  • Cave, anemone, shipwreck, vent: +40 when all 6 neighbours match its edges, +15 instead when all 6 are placed but at least one doesn't match
  • The goals card shows your points by colour

Goals give stars

  • Each goal met = 1 ★ star
  • Tap a goal to light up your best zone for it
  • Stuck? 💡 suggests a spot: 5 hints per reef, 3 on hard levels

The pearl

  • Reach the level's points target to win its ● pearl
  • 3 ★ stars + ● pearl = mastered level, marked with a crown

Fish move in

  • Some layouts attract fish
  • Each new species joins your collection
  • The fish collection shows what each species needs to move in

Unlock chapters

  • A ★ star on every level of a chapter opens the next one
  • Every 10th level (red frame) is hard: bigger zone goals, more points for the pearl
  • Each chapter adds a twist, explained on its page
