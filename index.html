Grow a coral reef, one hexa at a time

Hexacoral is a calm puzzle game for Android. Place hexagonal tiles, match their edges to grow coral, kelp, sand and rock zones, and watch fish move in when their homes are ready.

What's inside

  • A short tutorial to start: four tiny reefs, one idea each
  • Hundreds of levels in themed chapters: storms, shipwrecks, treasure, sharks, oil spills, a volcano, the dark abyss…
  • A fish collection to fill: each species has its own favourite reef layout
  • Stars, pearls and crowns for every level, plus endless free dives
  • Hints when you're stuck, and undo when you change your mind
  • English and French

Free, no ads, no tracking

Hexacoral is free and has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts. It doesn't collect any data: your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
