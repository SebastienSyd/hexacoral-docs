Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

In short

Hexacoral does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, fish collection, statistics) and your settings are saved in a file on your phone, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: a short buzz when you place a hexa. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other permission.

Links to other sites

The Support button opens the developer's Buy Me a Coffee page in your web browser. That site has its own privacy policy; Hexacoral shares nothing with it.

Children

Hexacoral collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
